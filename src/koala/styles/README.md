# Bundled citation styles

These Citation Style Language files were retrieved on 2026-10-02 from the community-maintained CSL styles repository:

- MLA Handbook, 9th edition: https://github.com/citation-style-language/styles/blob/master/modern-language-association.csl
- Chicago Manual of Style, 18th edition, author–date: https://github.com/citation-style-language/styles/blob/master/chicago-author-date.csl

The files contain their original authorship, contribution, and license notices. They are licensed under **Creative Commons Attribution–ShareAlike 3.0**, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/ . KOALA's MIT license does not replace these notices or cover these third-party styles.

Citation processing uses Pandoc through pypandoc_binary. Pandoc is GPL-licensed; pypandoc is MIT-licensed. Pandoc runs as a separate executable and is installed as a dependency; its upstream notices apply. Public distributions must retain the applicable licenses and satisfy redistribution requirements for their dependencies.


KOALA modifications (2026-10-05): MLA explicitly quotes short-work titles even when
container metadata is absent, and labels poetry line locators. The modified MLA
style remains licensed under CC BY-SA 3.0, with upstream notices retained.
Rules checked against https://owl.purdue.edu/owl/research_and_citation/mla_style/mla_formatting_and_style_guide/mla_in_text_citations_the_basics.html
and https://www.chicagomanualofstyle.org/tools_citationguide/citation-guide-2.html .
